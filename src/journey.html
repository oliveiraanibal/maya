Maya: journey map and task flow

The family dinner run in ten beats, from sign-in to the empty car charging after its errand. Michael shares needs and goals; Maya does the rest, speaks only when she needs him, and shows everything else on the Central Display and Panoramic Vision. Three reroutes happen on the way (default to plan, pharmacy, pickup), plus one courier errand that the car runs on its own.

Journey map

Scroll sideways on small screens. Emotion is Michael's expected feeling at each beat (1 low, 5 high). Screen IDs (S01 to S20, C1 to C5) come from the Screen Specs; A2 to A16 are assumptions from Concept Foundations. The last rows are the service blueprint: what Maya does backstage and what the outside world does.

Task flow

The decision logic behind the ten beats: sign-in, the briefing, the three kinds of replan (world, conversation, Michael), the courier errand and the hand-off to the empty car.
