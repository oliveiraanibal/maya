UX challenge · New AI assistant for infotainment

Maya

L5 trip concierge

A self-driving car frees your hands, not your agenda. Navigation handles routes, not commitments, so passengers still sequence the stops, phone businesses and replan whenever something changes.

With Maya, you share your needs and goals and she plans the stops, confirms with the shops, pays from her wallet and adapts when conditions change. Everything is shown on your central display and Panoramic Vision.

When the car is free, she becomes the courier that runs your errands.
