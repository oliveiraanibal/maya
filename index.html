Maya

An AI trip concierge for any Level 5 car

A self-driving car frees your hands, not your evening. Navigation handles routes, not commitments, so passengers still sequence the stops, phone businesses and replan by hand whenever something changes.

With Maya, you share your needs and goals; she plans the stops, phones the shops, pays from her wallet and adapts when the world or a conversation changes the plan. Everything shows on the central display and Panoramic Vision, and everything can be undone. When the car is free, she sends it to run your errands.

The story follows Michael, a father of two, from work to a 19:00 family birthday dinner: three pickups, a prescription, flowers on the table, and a suit the empty car collects while the family eats.
