How to use this prototype

The story follows Michael through one evening. Every step has two ways in: touch (tap the screen) or voice (the big button at the bottom, which plays what Michael would say).

  • Tap anywhere on the screen to see which parts are live.
  • Show taps keeps them highlighted.
  • “What if…” buttons open the edge cases, then take you back.
  • Keys: ← back, → or Space continue, T taps, M screens, R restart.

Designers: press E to edit tap zones. See the README in the repo.

Edit zones · drag to move, corner to resize, Alt-drag to draw, Delete removes, double-click sets the target
