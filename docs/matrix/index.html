Multimodal Matrix

Every task can be done by voice alone or touch alone; Maya's speech always has captions, and every chime has a visual twin.

Principles

  1. Parity: every task can be completed by voice alone or by touch alone. A single-mode step is a bug.
  2. Mirroring: everything Maya says appears as a caption, and every chime has a visual twin.
  3. The strip carries nothing unique. Anything shown there is also on the tablet, and it cannot be touched.
  4. Voice is for intent, touch is for precision (drag, pick, correct), but each can do the other's job.
  5. No time pressure on undo or correction, so people who are slower to speak or tap are not penalised.
  6. Only the signed-in user commands Maya. Other occupants' speech is context only, and edits on the tablet are tied to the signed-in user.
  7. Without you, the phone is the window. When the car runs an errand alone, nobody is in the cabin to see the tablet or hear Maya, so the run is followed and reported on Michael's phone: view only, through notifications, never by voice.

Stress tests

Situation What it exposes Fix
Touch-only user (speech-impaired) Ambient intent only hears others, never them They instruct by touch; ambient intent still works on calls and messages
Voice-only user (motion-impaired) Undo and edits need a tap Every action has a spoken command, and undo has no short timeout
Noisy cabin Voice misses or errors After one failed attempt, Maya shows the touch path on the tablet instead of asking again
Hard of hearing The chime is missed Visual twin on both the tablet and the strip, and optional haptics
Children tapping the tablet Accidental edits Edit actions tied to the signed-in user's profile
Privacy (silent mode) The user does not want to speak aloud Full touch path, and Maya's speech becomes captions only
Nobody in the car (courier mode) No one sees the tablet or hears Maya, and the shop staff are not users The phone is the window, view only; the staff get a code by text and one flashing door, and nothing else in the car responds to them

Decisions this adds to the plan

  • Maya's lines always have captions on the tablet.
  • Undo has no countdown.
  • The strip is read-only and a copy of the tablet, and it is off when the cabin is empty.
  • Voice and touch both stay available at every moment, with no mode switch. There is no mic button: tapping Maya's bar opens the keyboard.
  • In courier mode the phone, view only, replaces the tablet, and the shop staff only ever get a code and one door.
